who we are

Hello, Stranger

In the world of Tantra, we are Hiroshima and Irida, it's our spiritual names. But we are always called simply Sima and Ira. We are partners in real life, business partners, kindred spirits and the creators of our tantric rituals. We have been together for almost nine years, connected not only by love but also by a shared purpose: exploring the body, emotions, and life itself.

Over the years we have guided more than 900 sessions, and our practice has expanded far beyond Ukraine: Copenhagen, Kishinev, Berlin, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Bucharest, Helsinki and Vienna.

Together we bring medical education, experience in Wellness SPA, body-oriented therapy, Chinese and Korean medicine, chakra theory, and digital psychology. All of this has become the foundation of our work — helping people through the body, genuine connection, and energy.

In this are, we are a "blue ocean". Our sessions are so unique that they cannot be put on a par with other offers. They have no analogues, they cannot be copied, matched or replaced.

our journey

Over the years we have traveled a remarkable path together — from jealousy, the fear of losing each other, and discomfort when talking about intimacy, to consciously embracing our own sexuality. Now we help others do the same: to free themselves from shame, outdated beliefs, and physical tension, allowing desire to become not something to fear, but a source of genuine vitality.

We wanted our relationship to feel alive — not formal, not built on control. That search became the beginning of everything: first between the two of us, and later between us and the people who come into our space.

philosophy

To us, every woman is...

A symbol of limitless strength and passion, so often hidden beneath the expectations of a patriarchal world. A goddess living among chaos, war, destruction, and injustice — yet still capable of believing in a better world.

Social conventions, stereotypes, and simplistic labels have never been and never will be part of our world.

We deeply admire the female body — its beauty, its curves, its sensitivity, its sensuality, and the extraordinary way women are able to open themselves through touch.

If you come to one of our sessions, your sexual orientation simply doesn't matter to us. Whether you've ever experienced intimate touch with another woman or not, you are already beautiful in our eyes because of one thing: your courage to stay alive, curious, and willing to explore new horizons.

The Experience of Receiving Care

Most women's practices teach the same things:

– take care of yourself
– love and accept yourself
– awaken your femininity

Our Tantra Ritual is about something different. It is about allowing yourself to RECEIVE care from other people. That is a completely different psychological experience. For many women, accepting care is far more difficult than giving it.
