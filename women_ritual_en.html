What is Mnushi?

Mnushi is not a massage but a practice of C-tactile neurorelaxation. It is based on a special quality of touch that gently influences the nervous system through a feeling of safety and human connection.

Mnushi is closer to the art of therapeutic touch and relaxation than to massage. The practice is performed through a thin layer of fabric rather than directly on bare skin.

What is Pulsing?

Pulsing is a body practice based on gentle rhythmic rocking. While supporting the body with our hands, we create soft wave-like movements that may involve the entire body or individual parts of it.

The rhythm itself influences the nervous system, helping it settle into a state of deep relaxation. The practice is performed through thin fabric, without oils or deep pressure.
